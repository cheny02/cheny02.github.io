About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), supervised by Prof. Yu Zhang. My research focuses on transfer learning, prompt tuning for vision-language models, and adversarial robustness, with an emphasis on improving generalization and robustness in multi-modal systems.
